Rob's Home Automation Journey

and why it's a pickle that threw me down the rabbit hole...


It started with a lure...


Since I was little, I was fascinated by how technology can interact with humans based on external elements. In the world of 8bits amd MS-DOS run PCs, I created silly project of several scripts how a PC can greet me based on week day, time of the day etc. and respond to my simple messages. Fast forward more than two decades I moved to a new old house where I finally get to play with smart home gadgets. Not uncommonly, I started with pre-build Chinese cloud-based gadgets like [insert any chinese smart home brand].

---Several systems, same mission---

Xiaomi & Aqara devices
Bulb lights and door/windows sensors with alarm.

    They were
  • Cheap (mostly)
  • Fairly reliable

    • but also

  • often require internet connection
  • App not great
  • Limited device options
  • Out of region cloud placement

Sonoff/eWelink devices

    Are
  • also failry cheap
  • also fairly reliable
  • Wifi only (at start) with it's own limitation

    • but

  • reliability varies from product to product
  • Not all devices work as expected
  • Limited device options
  • Out of region cloud placement

Other devices

    Various other devices wich I added for different use cases over time, including
  • Tuya/SmartLife - Wifi
  • Shelly - z-wave mostly
  • Tapo - internal wifi cameras
  • Reolink - external POE cameras
  • Gree - A/C
  • Tahoma - shaders

  • Hue - bulbs and remotes
